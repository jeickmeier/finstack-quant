<DeskContext question={"The PM has five minutes. What should they do, and which evidence supports that decision?"}>

A report is the final stage of an analytical process. It must preserve dates, units, model assumptions and reconciliations while making the decision easy to find. In this lesson the same native results produce a detailed report pack, an exact reconstruction from public presentation primitives and a compact six-KPI PM brief.

Complete two sessions: assemble the traceable evidence pack, then design and challenge the decision brief. The report date is 15 October 2025. The performance history covers the three earlier observation periods. The loan introduced in 4.3 is acquired at the report date using settled cash; that disclosed acquisition prevents a later holding from being silently inserted into historical returns.

</DeskContext>

<Standard>

Start with the decision and its conditions, then show the minimum evidence needed to assess it. NAV explains capital at risk; TWR separates investment performance from external subscriptions; XIRR describes the investor's experience. A qualified rates sensitivity and credit sensitivity describe different exposures. A full repricing stress supplies a currency loss and its fraction of NAV. These six KPIs serve distinct purposes. A dashboard with more precision or more metrics is not automatically more informative.

Traceability requires an unbroken path from fixture to calculation to output. Keep full precision for checks and apply display formatting only at the last step. Money identities use serialized decimal amounts and a stated one-cent tolerance. The exact self-financing identity is native positions plus settled cash before and after purchasing the loan. Repricing stress holds settled cash constant; report-date cash is a ledger balance, not another live deposit position.

The evidence pack contains different windows and methods. Three-period account performance is historical within this synthetic fixture. The January–February attribution is a separately dated methodological example. The risk report uses the synthetic covariance panel and a fixed risk-capital denominator, while the stress report uses actual native revaluation of the current holdings. These outputs must retain their labels. In particular, a model VaR is not a realized return, a Taylor residual is not alpha and an unknown liquidity estimate is not zero.

The reporting package is a pure presentation layer. `TearSheet`, `Section` and `KPI` compose the convenience functions. A primitive reconstruction should preserve numerical results exactly. The performance helper currently supplies a static “Decimal mode” label; replace it with verified arithmetic and observation metadata before rendering. Presentation text does not establish calculation precision. Charts need an explicit origin, units and observation window. Use restrained colour for meaning, remove decoration and explain missing or undefined values rather than hiding them.

</Standard>

### Session 2: choose the PM's six numbers

Choose account NAV, linked TWR, annualized investor XIRR, sum of qualified USD-OIS bucketed DV01, qualified ACME-HZD CS01 and the full repricing rates-stress P&L. TWR and XIRR answer different questions and carry different annualization labels. The bucket sum and qualified credit key are selected exposures, not a claim to summarize every currency, curve or option Greek. Keep the full risk inventory in the detailed pack. The bucket sum is a local sensitivity summary under the native tenor bump convention; it need not exactly equal a finite parallel-zero shock. Serialized aggregate entries contain a `total` and entity subtotals; use the numerical `total` field.
