## Accepted decision for checked directional and mixed PDE stencils

1. **Use the backward pricing generator and the prepared spatial grid.**
   In forward model time \(t\), with terminal data at \(T\), write
   \(\partial_t U+L(t)U+f(t)=0\). The positive step in time to
   maturity uses \(L(T-\tau)\) and \(f(T-\tau)\) for
   \(\tau=T-t\). Sample callbacks at the actual forward model time
   supplied by the time marcher. In 1D the generator is
   \(aU_{xx}+bU_x+cU\). In 2D it is
   \(a_xU_{xx}+a_yU_{yy}+mU_{xy}+b_xU_x+b_yU_y+cU\).
   Coefficients multiply the indicated derivatives in the grid
   coordinates. \(m\) is the full multiplier of \(U_{xy}\).
   Use the stored knots and actual adjacent widths of
   [011](pde-grids-interpolation-and-greeks.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-pde-grids-interpolation-and-coordinate-greeks).

2. **Admit one checked coefficient sample per interior node and time.**
   Use coherent, fallible coefficient evaluation at the node's
   coordinates and actual stage time. Sample each required coefficient
   once and share it across the directional and mixed rows. Require
   finite values, with nonnegative directional diffusion. Finite signed
   drift, reaction, source and mixed coefficient are valid, subject to
   the diffusion constraint below. Propagate callback errors. Validate
   every required coefficient even if another term vanishes. A declared
   zero source remains valid.

3. **Require positive semidefinite two-dimensional diffusion.** Test
   the exact represented coefficients for
   \(m^2\le 4a_xa_y\). The matrix has off-diagonal \(m/2\).
   Equality is admitted, and either zero diagonal requires \(m=0\).
   Use a scale-safe exact comparison of the finite binary64 inputs,
   including subnormal and large coefficients. Reject violations
   without a tolerance, projection, correlation clamp or inferred
   diffusion. Coefficients generated by a bridge must be constructed
   coherently within this contract or fail explicitly. Bridge parameter
   admission and conversion are owned by
   [017](black-scholes-and-heston-pde-bridges.md).

4. **Use three-point nonuniform central directional differences.**
   For actual positive widths \(h_-\), \(h_+\), let
   \(H=h_-+h_+\). Use the quadratic first derivative
   \[
   D_1U=\frac{h_+ (U_0-U_-)/h_-+h_- (U_+-U_0)/h_+}{H}
   \]
   and curvature
   \[
   D_2U=2\frac{(U_+-U_0)/h_+-(U_0-U_-)/h_-}{H}.
   \]
   In the central branch the lower and upper coefficients of
   \(aD_2+bD_1\) are
   \(q_-=(2a-bh_+)/(h_-H)\) and
   \(q_+=(2a+bh_-)/(h_+H)\). The same directional rule
   serves 1D and both directions of 2D.
   [Directional difference references](../../../REFERENCES.md#checked-directional-and-mixed-pde-stencils)

5. **Switch convection locally at the exact off-diagonal limit.**
   For \(b\ge0\), use central convection when \(bh_+\le2a\).
   For \(b<0\), use it when \((-b)h_-\le2a\).
   Equality stays central and gives the affected coefficient exactly
   zero. Compare the represented inputs without overflow, underflow
   or a tolerance that moves the switch. Otherwise keep the same
   curvature and use \((U_+-U_0)/h_+\) for positive drift or
   \((U_0-U_-)/h_-\) for negative drift. Equivalently, add
   \(\max(-b,0)/h_-\) and \(\max(b,0)/h_+\) to the two
   diffusion off-diagonals. Zero diffusion permits pure convection.
   Zero drift leaves pure diffusion. This local rule has no global
   Péclet cutoff.

6. **Apply the directional row and source once.** Before boundary
   elimination the directional operator is
   \(q_-(U_--U_0)+q_+(U_+-U_0)+c_d U_0+f_d\).
   The matrix diagonal is \(c_d-q_--q_+\). In 1D,
   \((c_d,f_d)=(c,f)\). In 2D build one tridiagonal x-line
   for each interior y and one tridiagonal y-line for each interior
   x. Each gets \(c/2\) and \(f/2\) from the same sample.
   The mixed term carries no part of either. Preserve the logical
   split through time-stage evaluation when detached halves would
   round away required contributions. The time march owns stage weights.

7. **Use the tensor product of central first differences for mixing.**
   Replace the four-corner secant on asymmetric grids with
   \(mD_y^{(1)}D_x^{(1)}U\), using the two axes' quadratic
   first-derivative weights from clause 4. This is the tensor sum
   over \(i-1,i,i+1\) and \(j-1,j,j+1\), with the
   x derivative applied first and the y derivative second or a
   stable equivalent. Use the centered weights even where directional
   drift selects one-sided differences. With both axes locally
   symmetric this reduces to the four corners. Zero \(m\) gives
   an exact zero mixed contribution after admission. Use bounded
   local work and actual node geometry.
   [Tensor stencil reference](../../../REFERENCES.md#checked-directional-and-mixed-pde-stencils)

8. **Keep mixed evaluation explicit and stage-consistent.** Apply the
   mixed operator to the complete boundary-inclusive field at the
   same stage and time as the coefficients. Use the old field at
   the old time and the reconstructed destination-stage field when
   the selected correction requires it. The mixed term is outside
   the implicit tridiagonal line solves. Use the edge and corner
   values produced by
   [013](pde-boundary-elimination.md).
   Theta marching, Thomas solves, ADI stage order and startup
   damping remain with
   [014](theta-time-march-and-thomas.md) and
   [016](mcs-adi-time-march.md).

9. **Keep geometry, samples and rows bound to the solve.** Reuse
   geometry for the same admitted grid. Reuse coefficient-dependent
   rows across times only when the existing homogeneous declaration
   guarantees that all cached coefficients and source are invariant
   for that problem, and the applicable boundary linear forms
   permit reuse. Refresh time-dependent boundary data under 013.
   Otherwise sample each required stage time. Use validated internal
   assembly and check all full, interior and line dimensions, indices
   and allocations. Remove debug-only shape assumptions and
   unvalidated raw operator state construction.

10. **Make construction and application fallible at the complete
    calculation scale.** Use checked signed products, differences
    and sums for branch comparisons, combined coefficients, affine
    application and mixed terms. Preserve genuine zero, such as a
    constant field's spatial derivatives. Large or subnormal data
    can require scale-aware evaluation before materializing a
    coefficient, difference, product or stencil weight. Where the
    required row, action or stage contribution remains unresolved,
    return a numerical error with applicable node, direction and
    time context. Invalid input, callback, shape and resource
    failures also propagate. Avoid width floors, clipped
    coefficients and partial successful solver outputs.

11. **State the scope of accuracy and stability.** Each direction's
    spatial off-diagonals are nonnegative before boundary
    elimination. The one-sided drift adds local numerical
    diffusion \(|b|h_{\mathrm{toward}}/2\) relative to the
    central drift and gives first-order convection accuracy.
    Directional three-point curvature can have first-order
    truncation error on asymmetric grids. For smooth data on the
    local stencil, the centered tensor mixed derivative is
    second-order consistent. Its real-arithmetic error is bounded
    by \((h_{x,-}h_{x,+}\sup|U_{xxxy}|+
    h_{y,-}h_{y,+}\sup|U_{xyyy}|)/6\) with the suprema over
    the stencil rectangle. This is a local spatial bound.
    Pointwise diffusion admissibility and directional signs alone
    cannot establish mixed-stencil positivity, full time-step
    stability or price and Greek convergence.

12. **Reconcile and qualify the existing product routes.** Apply
    these rules to 1D equity and continuous and discrete barriers,
    including direct stepper calls, and 2D Heston. Keep operator
    state Rust-only and propagate pricing failures through existing
    host routes. Independently check both drift signs and ties,
    pure and rank-deficient diffusion, scale extremes, nonlinear
    manufactured fields on asymmetric grids, boundary-inclusive
    stencils, time-varying source and cache equivalence. Qualify
    spatial, temporal and boundary errors separately against
    independent price and risk references, including low diffusion
    and strong mixed terms. Product model admissibility, payoff,
    exercise, market inputs and risk conventions stay with their
    owners.

## Observed choice and formulation

[operator.rs](../../../../finstack-quant/models/src/pde/operator.rs) discretizes L u=a u_xx+b u_x+c u+f over interior unknowns. For neighboring widths h−,h+, diffusion coefficients are (2a/(h−H),−2a/(h−h+),2a/(h+H)), H=h−+h+. Central first-derivative weights are (−h+/(h−H),(h+−h−)/(h−h+),h−/(h+H)).

Add the central drift stencil only when b≥0 and b h+≤2a, or b<0 and −b h−≤2a. Otherwise use a forward difference for b≥0 or backward difference for b<0, preserving the off-diagonal sign of this backward pricing operator. The local order drops in convection-dominated cells; there is no global Peclet rejection. Add c to the diagonal and f to the affine source.

## Two-dimensional selection and conventions

[operator2d.rs](../../../../finstack-quant/models/src/pde/operator2d.rs) constructs one x-line operator per interior y and one y-line operator per interior x. Each receives half the reaction and half the source, preventing double counting. Mixed coefficient a_xy is stored divided by (h_x−+h_x+)(h_y−+h_y+). Application multiplies the four corner combination u++−u+−−u−++u−−, with boundary-inclusive values. ADI treats this term explicitly, independently of the directional upwind switch.

## Limitations and alternatives

On arbitrary asymmetric nonuniform grids, the secant cross stencil is exact for bilinear xy but generally contains first-order terms proportional to unequal left/right widths. Smooth sinh refinement may improve behavior; it does not establish second-order accuracy for all accepted `from_points` grids. Negative/nonfinite diffusion coefficients and general PDE ellipticity are not validated by operator construction. Local monotonicity of directional terms is not a proof of full mixed-operator positivity or ADI stability. A tensor product of full nonuniform derivative stencils is an unimplemented alternative.

## Evidence

- For \(a=3,b=2,h_-=2,h_+=3\), the central tie gives
  \((q_-,q_+)=(0,2/3)\). The mirrored negative drift gives
  \((2/3,0)\). With \(a=b=2^{1023}\) and widths 1 and 3,
  both sides of the naive branch comparison overflow. The true
  branch is one-sided and has the representable row
  \((2^{1022},-2^{1023},2^{1022})\) at zero reaction.
- At the origin on axes \((-1,0,2)\) and \((-2,0,3)\),
  \(U=x^2y+xy^2+2xy\) has mixed derivative 2. The proposed
  tensor gives 2 and the four-corner secant gives 4. Halving
  the widths gives 3 for the old secant. For \(U=x^2y^2\),
  the tensor gives 0 and the old secant gives 1.
- On axes \((0,1,4)\), \((-1,2,4)\), use
  \(U=x^2y+3xy^2+2x-y+5\) with
  \((a_x,a_y,m,b_x,b_y,c,f)=(2,3,1,1,-1,-2,3)\).
  At \((1,2)\) the two directional contributions including
  the affine split are \(17/2\) and \(-23/2\). The mixed
  contribution is 14, giving exact full action 11. The old
  mixed stencil gives 13 and full action 10.
- For \(a_x=a_y=2^{800}\) or \(2^{-800}\) and
  \(m=4a_x\), diffusion is indefinite even though both
  squared-product sides overflow or underflow together.
  \((a_x,a_y,m)=(2^{-800},2^{800},2)\) is exact equality.
  For the existing Heston coefficient expressions with
  \(\rho=\operatorname{nextDown}(1)\),
  \(\sigma_v=0.9441473299836161\) and
  \(v=0.036718169527261216\), separate binary64 products yield
  an indefinite represented triple from an admissible mathematical
  covariance. This is a bridge construction case for 017.
- On symmetric axes with \(h=2^{-500}\), \(m=2^{100}\)
  and \(U=xy\), the mixed output is \(2^{100}\) even
  though the detached mixed weight overflows. At
  \(h=2^{500}\), \(m=2^{-100}\), the detached weight
  underflows while the output remains \(2^{-100}\).

`cross_derivative_of_product_on_non_uniform_grid` in [operator2d.rs](../../../../finstack-quant/models/src/pde/operator2d.rs) checks u=xy to 1e−10; it does not test nonlinear mixed derivatives. `mcs_step_handles_convection_dominated_grid_via_upwinding` in [adi.rs](../../../../finstack-quant/models/src/pde/adi.rs) and Heston high-κ tests are bounded examples. [MCS reference](../../../REFERENCES.md#in-t-hout-welfert-2009) supports the method family, not arbitrary-grid order or every coefficient regime.
