## Evidence and interpretation

Source establishes what runs; test names alone establish located coverage, not a passing result. The inspected two-bucket ECL test has a hand-computed unconditional-PD oracle. Several weighting comparisons reuse production outputs and therefore demonstrate internal consistency only. The local immediate/linear CECL reversion tests inspected here assert a positive allowance, which does not qualify numerical accuracy or grid invariance. Inline and integration tests for accounting identities, periods, currencies, missing values and branch selection are cited with those narrower roles.

The [IFRS 9](../../../REFERENCES.md#ifrs-9-impairment), [ASC 326](../../../REFERENCES.md#asc-326-cecl) and [BCBS ECL guidance](../../../REFERENCES.md#bcbs-2015-ecl-guidance) entries identify conceptual accounting frameworks. They do not prove that embedded thresholds, historical PD, CCF, cure counts or measurement approximations are mandated by a standard, current, empirically calibrated or approved for a reporting entity. No new financial method or policy is adopted by this documentation.

## Material cross-cutting observations

- A check marked passed may contain Warning/Info findings or no evaluated observations; several missing/non-finite inputs silently skip. Unit and finite-result validation differ across individual checks.
- Suite comments describe ratio trends while implementation trends debt and EBITDA levels; the implied-interest path requires per-period rates without annual-to-period scaling and silently uses current balance when prior data is absent.
- Credit summaries, DSCR and covenant adapters deliberately use different windows and definitions. The maturity wall groups current balances by final maturity rather than projecting principal cash due; numeric table export does not itself preserve the summary currency.
- ECL policy source labels are distinct from provenance for every numeric default. Stage/curve/policy/currency evidence is only partially carried into outputs.
- CECL linear reversion derives its boundary hazard over a grid-dependent interval and does not split at the reversion end. WARM dispatch ignores PD/scenario/discount/impairment inputs after common exposure validation. These choices need explicit scope and independent qualification.
