# STATEMENTS-EVALUATION-012 — Contractual cashflows, waterfall feedback and period snapshots

Decision status: **Accepted for the full capital-state orchestration and period snapshot contract (2026-11-15)**. Implementation status: **Reconciled (2026-11-17)**. One ordered period engine serves ordinary, prepared and Monte Carlo evaluation. Each period reads what is due from the instruments' schedules under the run's market and valuation date, reconciles opening principal to the schedule, and keeps scheduled obligations (`scheduled_*_due`), cash settlements and economic principal distinct. Before a run the cash pool, the prepayment and sweep sizing inputs and the PIK metric are traced through formulas and masks: a dependency on a same-period capital output is rejected with its chain, a lagged reference reads the committed prior period, and an elected ECF deduction reads what earlier priorities paid. A configured waterfall runs once per period, including a period with no claim. The owner then rebuilds the schedules of instruments whose principal or coupon election changed, native totals are aggregated first and consolidated at the period-close rate under an explicit policy (a missing quote leaves only the consolidated amount unavailable), and the formulas that read final capital amounts are evaluated once, on the same forecast shocks. The period works on a staged copy of the debt state: ledger, elections and rebuilt schedules replace the committed state only after those formulas have evaluated, so a period that fails leaves the last committed period, and later periods read the committed snapshot. A simultaneous cash-and-debt solve is not performed. Validation status: **Qualified** by the record's example (20 due, 12 eligible: 12 paid and 8 in arrears, with the next period's lag reading 12 and settling the 8); a four-quarter cash and debt bridge computed by hand for an amortizing loan with a sweep, including the close day earning on the pre-sweep balance; a loan issued on a period boundary with the waterfall running on no claim before it; a PIK metric that reads a same-period output through an alias; and the same model evaluated twice giving identical results. A zero-variance Monte Carlo path already equals the deterministic evaluation. Audit date: 2026-11-05. Source revision: `7e82fd03ad98346727769e88dd5ac5d84b56e9fc` ([slice audit](README.md)).

## Accepted decision for capital-state orchestration and period snapshots

1. Establish what is due using the selected run context and explicit market inputs where required. Reconcile opening principal to the canonical schedule. Apply funding, elections, accrual and principal events on their governing dates. Keep scheduled obligations, cash settlements and economic principal distinct. [Opening principal and accrual](../statements-capital/03-funding-and-balances.md) · [Dated principal claims](../statements-capital/04-dated-principal.md#accepted-decision-for-dated-principal-claims).
2. Trace the cash pool, elections and sizing inputs through formulas, masks and aliases. Admit committed prior-period values and inputs available by the relevant decision date. Reject dependencies on outputs of the stage that those inputs feed. An explicitly elected excess-cash-flow deduction can use payments an earlier priority has already made. Run a configured waterfall once per period, including periods with zero claims. [Waterfall allocation](../statements-capital/06-priority-allocation.md#accepted-decision-for-waterfall-cash-allocation-and-conservation) · [ECF deductions](../statements-capital/07-prepayments-and-ecf.md#accepted-decision-for-agreement-defined-prepayments-and-ecf-sweeps).
3. Rebuild affected schedules from authorized dated changes and aggregate native-currency amounts first. Cross-currency consolidation requires an explicit reporting currency and period-close FX. A missing reporting quote makes the affected consolidated amount unavailable and preserves native results. Reported COGS and operating expenses include the D&A already reported in those lines. [Residual schedule rebuilding](../statements-capital/05-residual-coupons.md#accepted-decision-for-residual-coupon-rebuilding) · [Reporting and FX](../statements-capital/09-reporting-and-fx.md#accepted-decision-for-capital-reporting-and-fx) · [Reported-cost earnings](../statements-contracts/07-earnings-and-margins.md#accepted-decision-for-reported-cost-earnings-and-margins).
4. Evaluate formulas that read final capital amounts once after allocation, using the same forecast shocks for that path. Validate the period and commit statement cells, provenance, capital snapshots and debt state together. Later periods read the committed snapshot. A simultaneous cash-and-debt solve requires its own decision. Ordinary, prepared and Monte Carlo evaluations share this ordered engine. [Prepared evaluation](01-graph-precedence-and-prepared-plans.md#accepted-decision-for-dependency-graphs-cell-precedence-and-prepared-evaluation).

For example, when Q1 cash interest due is \$20 and eligible cash is \$12, report \$12 paid and \$8 in arrears. A Q2 lag reads the \$12 payment. Preparation rejects a Q1 cash pool that reads its own payment through an alias.

## Audited scope and owned boundary

The evaluator integrates contractual instrument cashflows, statement formulas and an optional funding waterfall in a staged period loop. This record owns orchestration and aggregate observation semantics. Instrument adapters, debt accrual, residual schedule mutation and waterfall allocation are separately audited in [statements-capital](../statements-capital/README.md); those algorithms are not redefined here. Model-declared units and previously agreed reported-cost treatment remain in force: reported COGS/OPEX include their reported D&A; this audit does not introduce a second add-back in statement evaluation.

## Audited ordered algorithm

1. Build a capital integration with unique instrument IDs. Initialize debt opening balances from absolute raw-schedule outstanding strictly before period start. A positive funding event exactly at issue can make the opening balance zero; future issuance is zero, otherwise initial notional is the fallback. Derivatives start with zero debt notional.
2. For each period, rebuild/init residual debt state as required and delegate contractual period-flow calculation. Record instrument cashflow buckets, economic principal claims, closing balances and new funding. Aggregate contractual capital snapshots, then evaluate all statement nodes against them.
3. If a waterfall is configured, inspect the available-cash formula for direct debt-service feedback through the delegated guard, then execute the allocation using the evaluated available cash and current debt state. Replace affected instrument buckets and totals; set available/equity outputs and rebuild residual schedules at the final included day `period.end-1`.
4. Find formulas or masks that read capital references, then include their DAG dependents and reevaluate that set **once** against the post-waterfall snapshot. This is not a simultaneous fixed-point solve: the waterfall is not rerun, and the forecast cache remains intact. Period warnings are deduplicated before contractual warnings are appended.
5. Store the final snapshot as historical capital data, push statement results into history and advance instrument state for the next period. Lagged capital formulas subsequently read those recorded snapshots.

## Audited currency, signs and date conventions

Aggregate Money amounts first within native currency. With only one currency and no explicit reporting currency, aggregate in that native currency; with multiple currencies and no reporting currency, preserve currency-specific totals instead of inventing a common unit. The audit reported a default PeriodEnd policy. The current runtime uses the configured FX policy and rejects a missing policy for cross-currency conversion. [Current FX context](../../../../finstack-quant/statements/src/evaluator/capital_structure_runtime.rs).

The aggregate converter is supplied the same inclusive period-end snapshot date for each total. Even when policy is named `CashflowDate`, this caller does not supply individual cash dates. The agreed **period-close FX for waterfall allocations** is preserved. Builder prose promising per-cashflow conversion is a documentation/implementation-contract reconciliation item, not permission to change the allocation convention.

Ordinary/prepared/Monte Carlo evaluations use the default empty-market path when no explicit market API is invoked; fixed obligations may work but required curve/FX data fail. The public market evaluator supplies the actual context and as-of cutoff.

## Limitations and alternatives

One post-waterfall pass can change dependent ratios without re-solving the cash allocation that produced them. A fixed-point solver is a distinct financial model. The direct-feedback guard should not be described as proving the absence of every indirect cyclic economic dependency. Debt initialization takes magnitudes at this boundary, with instrument-specific sign/perspective treatment delegated to the capital scope.

The accepted orchestration uses the existing capital rules for cashflow and FX calculations. The audited direct guard and snapshot update require reconciliation for the accepted dependency and atomicity requirements.
