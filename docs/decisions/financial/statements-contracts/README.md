## Evidence boundaries

- Formula assertions and expected accounting identities demonstrate the implemented arithmetic under their supplied conventions. They are not independent approval of account definitions, credit thresholds, tax methods or financial-reporting compliance.
- Registry metadata does not enforce financial units or dependency completeness. Model-specific inference covers currency/scalar algebra; unresolved dimensions and some reference failures are deliberately deferred.
- Identity/formula checks can warn and skip periods while still reporting passed. Error materiality filtering is guarded, but a passing or empty suite is not proof of complete accounting coverage.
- Canonical serialization and schema fixtures establish representation properties. They do not prove evaluation provenance, market-data freshness or adoption of a financial method.
