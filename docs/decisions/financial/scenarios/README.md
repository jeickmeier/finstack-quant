Percentages use `10` for a +10% relative change; basis points use `1` for 0.0001 in decimal rates; correlation points use `0.01` for one correlation percentage point. Reports often describe requested changes rather than measured post-shock changes.

## Evidence and discrepancy boundaries

Two observed cross-boundary discrepancies affect interpretation: [base-correlation bucket detachment units](10-correlation-shocks.md) disagree with core/tranche percentage coordinates, and [partial mutation after failure](02-execution-and-partial-success.md) can differ between native pre-generated replacement batches and serial/WASM application. Forecast value-map success counts, recorded-only instrument overrides, no-match buckets and matrix no-ops also limit the meaning of `operations_applied`. These are distinct from unresolved choices about stress severity or accepted approximation error.

Formula/invariant tests, same-kernel replay checks and configuration snapshots are identified by evidence role in each record. They do not certify historical template calibration, all-surface arbitrage, pricing correctness, scenario completeness or a financial convention's adoption. Concrete open items are consolidated in the [scenario follow-ups](../FOLLOW_UPS.md#scenarios).
