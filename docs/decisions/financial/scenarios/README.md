## Evidence and discrepancy boundaries

[Base-correlation bucket detachment units](10-correlation-shocks.md) disagree with core/tranche percentage coordinates at the audit revision. The live engine stages market/date through shocks but publishes them before statements finish. Full atomic publication and serial error order are required under the [accepted execution contract](02-execution-and-partial-success.md#accepted-decision-for-ordered-and-atomic-scenario-execution). Observed forecast value-map success counts, recorded-only instrument overrides, no-match buckets and matrix no-ops also limit the meaning of `operations_applied`.

Formula/invariant tests, same-kernel replay checks and configuration snapshots are identified by evidence role in each record. They do not certify historical template calibration, all-surface arbitrage, pricing correctness, scenario completeness or a financial convention's adoption. Concrete open items are consolidated in the [scenario follow-ups](../FOLLOW_UPS.md#scenarios).

Shared boundaries: [core market lifecycle](../core-market/context-lifecycle.md), [cashflow fixing materialization](../cashflows/16-fixing-materialization.md), [curve/risk methodology](../core-market/rate-shocks.md), and [attribution](../attribution/README.md).
