## Evidence and discrepancy boundaries

At the audit revision, the base-correlation adapter used detachment units that disagreed with core/tranche coordinates. The accepted correction and whole-candidate checks await production qualification. [SCENARIOS-010](10-correlation-shocks.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-scenario-correlation-shocks).

The audited engine stages market/date through shocks and publishes them before statements finish. Full atomic publication and serial error order are required. Observed forecast value-map successes, recorded-only instrument overrides, no-match buckets and matrix no-ops also limit the meaning of `operations_applied`. Instrument and statement reconciliation require actual changes to the staged inputs and separate evidence for valuation or evaluated statement output. [SCENARIOS-002](02-execution-and-partial-success.md#accepted-decision-for-ordered-and-atomic-scenario-execution) · [SCENARIOS-011](11-instrument-overrides.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-scenario-instrument-overrides) · [SCENARIOS-012](12-statement-bindings.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-scenario-forecast-shocks-and-rate-bindings).

Formula/invariant tests, same-kernel replay checks and configuration snapshots are identified by evidence role in each record. They do not certify historical template calibration, all-surface arbitrage, pricing correctness, scenario completeness or a financial convention's adoption. Concrete open items are consolidated in the [scenario follow-ups](../FOLLOW_UPS.md#scenarios).
