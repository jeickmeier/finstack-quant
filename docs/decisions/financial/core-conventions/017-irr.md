# CORE-CONVENTIONS-017: Unique-return IRR/XIRR with log-growth Brent solving

- Decision status: **Accepted — complete IRR/XIRR package, 2026-11-07**.
- Implementation status: **Reconciled 2026-11-10 (G1); independent qualification pending.** `irr(cashflows)` and `xirr(flows)` take no guess or day count; periodic offsets shift in integer space and must be exact `f64`; XIRR uses actual days over 365 from the first surviving nonzero net date, netting each date once (ascending absolute value, then signed value, Neumaier); one sign change is required; the full `log1p` bracket `next_up(-1)..=f64::MAX` is solved by the shared Brent kernel through a fallible `solve_in_bracket` (100 iterations, `1e-12`); endpoints map to their finite rates; every success path passes one gate (finite in-bracket candidate, finite `r > -1`, recomputed finite `|rho| <= 1e-8`); residual failures are errors and a returned zero is unsigned. Consumers: portfolio MWR and structured credit use canonical XIRR; loans quote yields and EIR on an explicit `yield_basis` (ACT/365F or ACT/360, exact growth conversion, resolution O11); bond XIRR floors are ACT/365F only; PE and real-estate IRRs quote on their configured day count only when it is ACT/365F or ACT/360; the Monte Carlo per-path IRR is removed.
- Validation status: **Evidence inspected; independent arithmetic examples passed; production qualification pending**. Documentation links/status/counts and independent periodic/leap-XIRR, excluded-root, finite-endpoint, net-overflow and log-rounding examples passed. Linked Rust/host tests were located or inspected, not run; this does not certify the current solver or product behavior.

## Choice, scope and rationale

This record owns the supported scalar IRR/XIRR cash stream, timing convention,
preprocessing, unique-root eligibility, log-growth solver and numerical acceptance.
[Accepted 0044](../conventions/0044-financial-perspectives-and-boundary-conversions.md)
owns economic cash perspective; [015](015-cashflow-semantics.md#accepted-decision-cash-settlement-principal-effects-and-accrual-metadata)
owns settlement-cash versus balance events. [012](012-money-decimal-precision.md#accepted-decision-monetary-precision-rounding-and-currency-identity)
and [016](016-discounting.md#accepted-decision-dated-npv-selection-arithmetic-and-continuous-discounting)
retain monetary/model boundaries. Frequency-based analytics, dated CAGR,
instrument accrual and quoted yields do not select this XIRR clock. The package
below settles the remaining choices without certifying current solver execution
or adopting an instrument's separately owned yield/return-floor convention.

## Accepted decision: IRR and XIRR timing, eligibility and numerical acceptance

Accepted on **2026-11-07** after whole-record review. Runtime implementation
and independent production/host qualification remain pending;
observed behavior is retained separately below.

1. **Return effective growth rates from explicit economic cash.** Solve
   `sum(C_i/(1+r)^t_i)=0` for finite binary64 `r>-1`, returned as a decimal rate,
   not percent/bp or a continuously compounded rate. Internal `log1p(r)` is a
   numerical coordinate, not the returned compounding convention. Amounts are
   finite signed settlement cash in one owner-selected currency/unit system,
   positive receipts and negative payments, including owner-qualified projected
   cash. Noncash PIK/write-down principal is not a cash receipt under 015. Dietz
   external contributions require 0044's explicit sign conversion. No valuation-
   date cutoff/include_today, automatic initial/terminal cash, reinvestment rate,
   FX conversion or entitlement generation is performed here: use the whole
   supplied investment path, including dates before any reporting valuation date.

2. **Keep periodic IRR truly per input period.** `irr(cashflows)` assigns the
   original integer index to each amount; zero entries preserve elapsed periods.
   Remove zero amounts for solving without compressing their spacing. Shift by
   the first nonzero index in checked integer space, then require exactly
   representable binary64 time offsets; do not silently merge huge integer times
   by `usize as f64`. The owner supplies a regularly spaced period grid and its
   financial meaning. Return a per-period effective rate without frequency/date
   inference or automatic annualization; it is annual only for annual inputs.
   The representation check is not an arbitrary financial row-count cap.

3. **Make canonical XIRR fixed ACT/365F.** `xirr(flows)` uses the actual integer
   calendar days from the first surviving nonzero net date divided by exactly
   365. Leap days remain; no holiday adjustment, trading-day count, fiscal year,
   coupon frequency/reference period or inferred observation factor enters it.
   Return an effective annual 365-day-basis rate. Remove the public `day_count`
   argument, default DayCountContext, context-dependent convention failures and
   distinct-date equal-time aggregation. Do not add a context-aware alternate
   XIRR or synthetic-day-count registry. This is an explicit new XIRR choice,
   not something previously approved by the CAGR/accrual decisions. It shares
   Microsoft's documented 365-day timing convention without claiming Excel's
   guesses, root selection, date serials, errors or numerical outputs.

4. **Net each XIRR date once, with a defined binary64 model rule.** Validate
   all amounts before availability/netting shortcuts and normalize signed zero.
   Sort dates chronologically. Within each date, sort finite components by
   ascending absolute amount, then signed `total_cmp` value, and use checked
   shared Neumaier accumulation in that canonical order. Reject nonfinite
   intermediates/net amounts; do not replace them with zero. Keep one rounded
   net amount per date and remove exact computed zero nets before selecting
   the time origin. Use precisely that net stream for eligibility, objective
   and final residual; discard the original component lists instead of mixing
   one netting rule for classification with another stream for solving.
   This is deterministic preprocessing, not exact-real/ledger cancellation or
   a universal scale/sign-flip success guarantee. A small residue can be affected
   by rounding and a finite exact net may still fail the selected intermediate
   domain. Owners requiring exact monetary netting retain 012's path before
   deliberately entering the scalar model boundary.

5. **Require one nonzero sign change over distinct times.** After the specified
   preprocessing, require at least two nonzero cash groups at strictly increasing
   finite times and exactly one sign reversal, in either investment or borrowing
   direction. The selected real-valued net stream then has exactly one root
   with `r>-1`: choosing a time between the sign blocks makes the NPV multiplied
   by the corresponding exponential strictly monotone, with opposite limiting
   signs. This does not guarantee that the root is representable or passes the
   finite numerical solver. Reject more-than-one-switch streams as outside this
   supported contract even when a particular one has a unique admissible root;
   do not label every such stream mathematically ambiguous. No multi-root scan,
   nearest-guess root, MIRR, Newton fallback or automatic root-selection policy.

6. **Make underdetermination and unsupported endpoints explicit errors.** Empty,
   all-zero, one nonzero point, one surviving date, one-sign-only and fully
   cancelled streams do not identify a supported return; never return zero/NaN
   as a convenience result. An exact total wipeout such as periodic `[-100,0]`
   is not a finite `r>-1` IRR; do not import analytics' terminal-loss reporting
   convention into this solver. Validate inputs before these shortcuts, with
   typed Date admission at the caller boundary. There is no scalar currency check;
   mixed-unit/currency input remains invalid financial preparation, not something
   an apparent mathematical root repairs.

7. **Remove the public guess and implicit 10% seed.** Neither `irr` nor `xirr`
   takes a guess or user solver settings. One-switch eligibility already selects
   the economic root; a guess is not a financial forecast or permission to pick
   among roots. Use the full supported log-growth bracket with the existing
   safeguarded Brent solver and fixed controls below. Do not retain an alternate
   guessed signature, compatibility default or hidden multiple-root path. Internal
   numerical probes do not change the supported stream or bypass final checks.

8. **Retain log-scaled evaluation, with explicit numerical failure.** Set
   `x=log1p(r)` and store each selected nonzero amount's sign/log magnitude.
   For `a_i=log(abs(C_i))-t_i*x` and `m=max(a_i)`, evaluate
   `rho(x)=sum(sign(C_i)*exp(a_i-m))/sum(exp(a_i-m))` using shared Neumaier
   accumulation for numerator and gross denominator. Check required inputs,
   intermediates, positive finite gross and finite residual; failures propagate,
   not a fabricated +infinity sign or NaN success. A scaled exponential term may
   round/underflow to zero as an ordinary floating approximation; this is not
   permission to delete raw cash by an epsilon. Max-log scaling avoids raw PV
   powers/overflow but does not preserve every tiny contribution or prove exact
   cancellation. Reuse the shared Brent kernel, reconciling a fallible fixed-
   bracket entry there rather than adding a private solver copy.

9. **Bracket the finite rate domain and handle rate endpoints directly.** Use
   `r_min=next_up(-1.0)`, `r_max=f64::MAX` and log bounds
   `log1p(r_min)..log1p(r_max)`. No arbitrary loss floor, positive return cap or
   expanding scan is selected. A computed-zero endpoint is only a candidate and
   must pass the common final gate. If finite nonzero endpoint residuals have
   the same sign, report failure to bracket a qualifying root in this numerical
   domain rather than returning a guessed rate. For endpoint candidates use
   their finite rate values directly; rounded `log1p(MAX)` followed by `expm1`
   can overflow and is not proof that MAX is unrepresentable. Convert interior
   candidates with `expm1`; failed conversion errors without clamping to a bound.

10. **Keep fixed operational solver controls and one acceptance gate.** Retain
    at most 100 Brent iterations and configured log-coordinate tolerance `1e-12`.
    The shared solver's current width test is half-bracket width at most
    `2*EPSILON*abs(x)+0.5*1e-12`, or exact computed objective zero; it is not an
    NPV residual tolerance or a certificate of true root error. Every endpoint,
    computed-zero and ordinary convergence path must return through one gate:
    finite candidate log coordinate in the bracket; finite returned `r>-1`;
    and a separately recomputed **finite** residual at `log1p(returned_r)` with
    `abs(rho)<=1e-8`, using the same prepared net stream. An `abs(rho)>tol` test
    alone is insufficient because NaN makes that comparison false. Exhaustion,
    invalid evaluation, unbracketed/failed conversion or residual rejection is an
    error, never a zero/last iterate/nearest endpoint fallback. Normalize returned
    signed zero; do not round the result to percentage/bp presentation precision.

11. **State accuracy and invariance limits honestly.** `1e-12` is the solver's
    operational log tolerance and `1e-8` a computed gross-PV residual criterion,
    not a uniform absolute/relative error in r or a verified exact-log-root bound.
    Same-date net rounding, integer-day/365 conversion, logarithms, scaled
    exponentials and returned-rate conversion are separate numerical boundaries.
    Equal rounded logarithms of distinct large cash magnitudes can give a computed
    zero despite a nonzero true return. Mathematical common-scale/sign/time-origin
    invariance does not imply bit identity or unchanged numerical success under
    all rescalings/sign flips or supported platforms. Independently qualify
    preprocessing/classification and returned-rate residual/conditioning against
    exact/high-precision references, including cancellation, near-minus-one and
    extreme positive returns. No arbitrary precision engine, new count cap,
    user-tolerance framework or unproved global rate-error guarantee is adopted.

12. **Reconcile investment returns versus product-specific yields explicitly.**
    Low-level inputs carry no currency; Money adapters must validate common units/
    currency and consciously use 012's model-view boundary rather than claim
    ledger-exact IRR. Owners select actual initial/terminal valuation cash and
    disclosure, projected cash and qualified source dates. Loan quoted yields,
    bond return floors, PE conventions and worst/candidate-return selection are
    separately owned; do not silently ignore a configured non-365 day count or
    mechanically relabel an instrument yield as canonical XIRR. Reconcile each
    actual contract with its owner, using an explicit growth conversion only
    where mathematically valid or its qualified product-yield method. Do not
    preserve the removed generic clock/guess behavior as private copies or an
    undocumented alternate core API. Error-to-unavailable reporting and candidate
    selection policies require their owner's documented meaning, not suppressed
    solver failures or a guessed substitute result. Remove automatic periodic
    IRR on flattened captured Monte Carlo payoff components: losing timestamps
    and omitting a complete investment path does not establish regular periods.
    Any separately offered diagnostic needs its own explicit, complete timed-cash
    contract; do not add another inferred approximation here.

13. **Propagate the complete Rust/caller contract in one working slice.** Update
    core signatures/re-exports/docs/tests/examples/benches, deterministic netting,
    fixed clock, shared fallible bracket support and common acceptance path,
    together with all portfolio/performance, PE, loan, bond return-floor and other
    actual consumers, including captured-path diagnostics. Retain the raw helpers' current Rust-only scope; update
    existing public host product docs/errors/parity only where their real surface
    changes, without redundant direct host IRR/XIRR APIs. Remove retired arguments,
    seed defaults, synthetic-time netting, stale multiple-root claims, unchecked
    numeric paths and bypasses without aliases/migrations. This acceptance does
    not certify financial comparability, solve nonconventional projects or execute
    implementation and independent production/solver qualification automatically.

## Observed algorithm

1. Periodic times are original integer indices, preserving gaps created by zero amounts. XIRR validates finite amounts, sorts dates and uses Neumaier summation to identify nonzero net dates; fully cancelled dates are removed before selecting the first date as time origin. It retains original components of surviving dates for subsequent compensated aggregation.
2. XIRR computes signed year fractions from that first date using **default** `DayCountContext`, sorts by resulting time, and nets all equal year fractions with compensated summation. Synthetic day counts can map distinct dates to the same time. Remove zero nets.
3. Shared solver rejects nonfinite times/amounts and fewer than two nonzero points, shifts times by first remaining time, and requires exactly one sign change. Zero-sign values are ignored. More than one sign change errors even if a particular polynomial happens to have only one real admissible root.
4. Use `x=ln(1+r)`. Store log absolute amount and sign. Evaluate each log-PV `a_i=ln|C_i|-t_i*x`, subtract max(a), then sum signed exponentials and gross absolute exponentials separately with Neumaier compensation. Return signed PV/gross PV as the relative objective; nonfinite max yields infinity.
5. Initial guess defaults 0.1 and must be finite >-1. Evaluate its log-growth objective; exact zero returns the guess. Otherwise its sign relative to first flow chooses which side of the full log-domain bracket to replace. Lower rate is `-1+EPSILON/2`, upper log-growth is `ln(f64::MAX)`. Equal nonzero endpoint signs error as unrepresentable root.
6. Brent solve in the bracket uses absolute log tolerance `1e-12` and maximum 100 iterations. Convert with `exp_m1`; require finite rate >-1 and recomputed relative residual <=`1e-8`, otherwise error. No Newton or arbitrary scan fallback is used here.

## Observed selection, conventions and controls

Caller supplies flows, optional guess and XIRR day count; solver tolerances/domain are internal constants. The guess narrows a unique-root bracket and never chooses an economic root among alternatives. Negative and very large positive returns are supported only when representable with the residual accuracy. Cashflow signs represent payments/receipts from one perspective; no currency or calendar is carried by raw f64 amounts.

## Observed limitations and failure

XIRR cannot supply a calendar/frequency/coupon period: Bus252, ACT365L and ACTACT ICMA fail on nonempty intervals requiring that context. `OneOne` collapses all nonorigin dates to one unit, and 30/360 can collapse distinct dates; aggregation is intentional. Exactly one sign change gives at most one admissible root but not representability within f64. It does not establish the economic comparability of mixed units/currencies or an Excel-compatible multiple-root policy.

## Observed alternatives and tradeoffs

Periodic versus dated timing is supported; nonconventional multi-root search, MIRR, caller-tunable precision or context-aware XIRR are unimplemented alternatives. Log scaling avoids raw notional overflow and makes the residual scale-free; a residual bound is not the same as a fixed error bound on r for poorly conditioned streams.
