# STATEMENTS-CAPITAL-007: Independent prepayment buckets and excess-cash-flow sweep

**Decision status:** Accepted for the full prepayment and excess-cash-flow sweep contract (2026-11-15).
**Implementation status:** Reconciled (2026-11-18) except a separately elected principal-effective date: the principal reduction takes effect at the close of the period in which the sweep is paid. The sweep is measured on the evaluated period and elects its payment date as a day lag from that period's close (`payment_lag_days`, owner resolution 2026-11-18). With a lag the measured amount is a dated obligation: it is paid in the period that contains its due date, oldest first, and what cash or eligible principal cannot pay is carried as arrears and reported as `carried`; without one the sweep is paid in the period it is measured and an unpaid amount is reported, not carried. Each prepayment is opted into with its priority: `mandatory_prepayment` and `voluntary_prepayment` are `{amount, facilities}` and the sweep is `ecf_sweep`. Every one elects its facilities, either `all_eligible` or a named list; a named facility must be a debt instrument of the capital structure, and a term loan with a call schedule cannot be elected because the waterfall repays at par only. The sweep formula is authored term by term: a cash measure, the deductions subtracted and the credits added, each a signed formula in the waterfall currency or one of the paid-debt-service terms (cash interest, fees, scheduled principal actually paid by earlier priorities, arrears included). Nothing is deducted unless elected. ECF is exact `Money`; the fraction in `[0,1]` applies to positive ECF and a non-positive ECF requests nothing. A negative mandatory or voluntary request, a missing or non-finite input and a sizing expression that reads a same-period `cs` output are rejected. Payment is the least of the request, the remaining cash and the elected facilities' economic principal net of reserves, by class and exact pro-rata. Each rung reports its requested, paid and unpaid amounts (`prepayments`); an unpaid request is not carried, because no dated contractual obligation establishes it. Sizing, payment and the principal reduction all fall in the reporting period being evaluated. The sections below the accepted decision describe the audited implementation.
**Validation status:** Qualified by a lagged sweep measured in one quarter, due in the next, half paid and its arrears paid first in the quarter after; and by the record's example (a measure of 95, deductions of 35, a 50% sweep requesting 30, 22 of cash and 18 of eligible principal: 18 paid, 12 unpaid, 4 left in the pool), elected-only deductions and credits with a negative ECF, a mixed term-loan and revolver allocation under `all_eligible` and under a named list, a real term loan and revolver prepaid through the evaluator with the recorded outcome, a request sized through a node that reads a same-period `cs` output, a request above the named facility's principal, a negative request, an unknown facility at run time and at model validation, a callable loan elected by name and by `all_eligible`, a zero-percentage sweep with and without its priority, an empty and a repeated facility list, a deduction that reads a same-period `cs` output and the wire form. Independent agreement examples with dated ECF remain pending. Reconciled to the hand ledger of the [independent qualification](../../../../finstack-quant/statements/src/capital_structure/tests/independent_qualification.rs) for an agreement-style definition (EBITDA less cash taxes, capital expenditure and the cash interest and scheduled principal actually paid, plus asset-sale proceeds; 50% swept senior first). The requested amount is the fraction of ECF rounded to the currency's minor unit.

## Accepted decision for agreement-defined prepayments and ECF sweeps

1. Opt in separately to each at-par mandatory prepayment, voluntary prepayment and excess-cash-flow (ECF) sweep. Execute each in its configured waterfall position. Model the agreement's eligibility and select named facilities or explicitly elect all eligible facilities. Require supported payoff terms for any applicable premium or other payoff economics. [Prepayment instrument scope](01-instrument-construction.md#accepted-decision-for-opt-in-waterfall-prepayments).
2. Elect the agreement's ECF formula and its measurement, trigger, payment and principal effective dates. Evaluate finite, signed inputs in the waterfall currency. Apply the elected fraction in `[0,1]` to positive ECF. Elect each deduction and credit explicitly. When a deduction or credit is based on paid debt service, use the current amounts actually allocated by earlier waterfall priorities. Reject missing or invalid inputs and negative mandatory or voluntary prepayment requests.
3. Limit payment by the request, remaining cash and eligible economic principal net of reserves. Use the accepted priority, dated claim and schedule replay policies. Report requested, paid and unpaid amounts. Carry an unpaid amount as arrears only when a dated contractual obligation establishes it. [Dated principal claims](04-dated-principal.md#accepted-decision-for-dated-principal-claims) · [Residual coupon rebuilding](05-residual-coupons.md#accepted-decision-for-residual-coupon-rebuilding) · [Waterfall allocation](06-priority-allocation.md#accepted-decision-for-waterfall-cash-allocation-and-conservation).

For example, an agreement-defined cash measure of \$95 with \$35 in elected deductions yields \$60 of ECF. A 50% sweep requests \$30. With \$22 remaining in the cash pool and \$18 of eligible principal after reserves, the waterfall pays \$18 and leaves \$4 in the pool.

## Observed ECF sizing and allocation

For the current period evaluate finite node/formula values in the waterfall currency. Omitted tax, capital expenditure and change-in-working-capital nodes contribute zero. They are subtracted with their supplied sign; negative taxes/capex/working-capital use increases ECF. EBITDA is required. Cash interest uses an explicit override if present, else interest **actually paid by earlier priorities**, including arrears, and is floored at zero.

`ECF = EBITDA − taxes − capex − ΔWC − max(cash_interest,0) − earlier_paid_scheduled_principal − earlier_paid_fees`.

`requested_sweep = max(ECF · sweep_percentage,0)` where percentage is finite and in `[0,1]`. Only scheduled principal in earlier Amortization payments is in this principal deduction; the independent mandatory/voluntary prepayment buckets are not automatically deducted as scheduled amortization. PIK and unpaid service consume no cash. If Interest/Amortization follows Sweep, its automatic deduction is zero and the later category has no reserved cash. This is a stack-dependent formula, not a universal lender definition of ECF.

At Sweep, apply only the configured ECF amount. An optional target must exactly match a contractual row; without one allocate against all staged principal capacities by class and pro rata. There is no runtime instrument-type filter in the allocation helper; model validation restricts eligible capital structures. The “all term loans” wording on the target field should not be read as filtering out an admitted revolver.

Mandatory/voluntary rungs separately evaluate their sizing expressions, floor negative requests to zero, and use the same generic allocation without an ECF target. For any prepayment, budget is `max(min(requested,remaining_cash),0)`, additionally bounded by each row's remaining economic principal. Record only actual allocated cash, reduce both available cash and capacity, and increment the corresponding audit component. Requests exceeding capacity leave unused cash for later rungs/equity.

## Observed controls and limitations

The corresponding priority and sizing input must be supplied together for each prepayment, including a zero-percentage ECF sweep. Missing target IDs and nonfinite values fail. All prepayments are at par without premiums, penalties, legal restricted-payment tests or OID accretion. Earlier principal cannot be repaid again, and no later payment can manufacture arrears against extinguished capacity. [Accepted prepayment selection](01-instrument-construction.md#accepted-decision-for-opt-in-waterfall-prepayments).

Real credit agreements often define addbacks, thresholds, leverage step-downs, exclusions, mandatory-prepayment credits and permitted carryforwards. Those are not inferred here. They can be represented partly in supplied formulas, but the fixed deduction/order contract still applies. Alternative reserving of future debt service would change seniority and cash timing.
