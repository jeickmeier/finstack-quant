## Adoption boundaries

- A named regulator, CSA template, CCP or SIMM version is not evidence of approval, current applicability or independent validation.
- [Parameter resolution](01-registry-and-defaults.md) and [validation](02-simm-parameter-validation.md) retain the actual fallback and domain differences; overlay data are not uniformly validated at every public boundary.
- [SIMM](11-simm-total-and-mpor.md) is a historical USD-only approximation with missing product/factor dimensions. [CCP fallback](14-ccp-external-and-proxy.md) is exposure times a heuristic rate. These are materially different provenance classes.
- [IM account terms](17-csa-selection-and-im-terms.md), [VM](18-variation-margin.md) and [repo flows](20-repo-margin-cashflows.md) have different signs, transfer filters and account scopes. Their totals cannot be combined without a stated financial interpretation.
- Confirmed static concerns include row-level concentration checking, incomplete override-domain guards, ordinary row-import validation gaps and calendar-day settlement diagnostics labelled as business days. These static observations do not establish numerical consequences or authorize contract changes.

The records own observed algorithms, evidence and material scope limits. The
[collateral and initial-margin register](../FOLLOW_UPS.md#margin-collateral-and-initial-margin)
owns adoption questions, implementation reconciliation, investigations and missing
qualification evidence; task details are maintained there rather than in this inventory.
