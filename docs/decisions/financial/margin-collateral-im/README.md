## Adoption boundaries

- A named regulator, CSA template, CCP or SIMM version is not evidence of approval, current applicability or independent validation.
- [Parameter resolution and terms](01-registry-and-defaults.md#accepted-decision-for-explicit-margin-parameter-snapshots-and-contractual-terms) require explicit immutable selection and common validation. [SIMM parameter admission](02-simm-parameter-validation.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-parameter-profiles-and-correlation-validation) requires complete tables and checked dependence within the selected profile.
- [SIMM sensitivity admission](03-sensitivity-inputs.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-sensitivity-identity-scaling-and-netting) requires compatible factor and FX bases for netting and preserves curvature expiry through weighting.
- [Ordinary IR delta and vega](04-simm-interest-rate.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-interest-rate-delta-and-vega-aggregation) use qualified yield subcurves, expiry-based vega and separate reference-horizon charges.
- [Qualifying-credit delta and vega](05-simm-qualifying-credit.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-qualifying-credit-delta-and-vega) distinguish exact factor netting from concentration grouping and preserve the linear residual charge.
- [Nonqualifying-credit delta and vega](06-simm-nonqualifying-credit.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-nonqualifying-credit-delta-and-vega) require dated classification and distinguish tranche concentration from classified correlation groups.
- [Equity delta and vega](07-simm-equity.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-equity-delta-and-vega) require dated factor classification, a bucket-bound volatility proxy and explicit residual addition.
- [SIMM](11-simm-total-and-mpor.md) is a historical USD-only approximation with missing product/factor dimensions. [CCP fallback](14-ccp-external-and-proxy.md) is exposure times a heuristic rate. These are materially different provenance classes.
- [IM account terms](17-csa-selection-and-im-terms.md), [VM](18-variation-margin.md) and [repo flows](20-repo-margin-cashflows.md) have different signs, transfer filters and account scopes. Their totals cannot be combined without a stated financial interpretation.
- Confirmed static concerns include row-level concentration checking, incomplete override-domain guards, ordinary row-import validation gaps and calendar-day settlement diagnostics labelled as business days. These static observations do not establish numerical consequences or authorize contract changes.

owns adoption questions, implementation reconciliation, investigations and missing
