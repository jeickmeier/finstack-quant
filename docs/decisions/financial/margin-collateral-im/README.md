## Adoption boundaries

- A named regulator, CSA template, CCP or SIMM version is not evidence of approval, current applicability or independent validation.
- [Parameter resolution and terms](01-registry-and-defaults.md#accepted-decision-for-explicit-margin-parameter-snapshots-and-contractual-terms) require explicit immutable selection and common validation. [SIMM parameter admission](02-simm-parameter-validation.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-parameter-profiles-and-correlation-validation) requires complete tables and checked dependence within the selected profile.
- [SIMM sensitivity admission](03-sensitivity-inputs.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-sensitivity-identity-scaling-and-netting) requires compatible factor and FX bases for netting and preserves curvature expiry through weighting.
- [Ordinary IR delta and vega](04-simm-interest-rate.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-interest-rate-delta-and-vega-aggregation) use qualified yield subcurves, expiry-based vega and separate reference-horizon charges.
- [Qualifying-credit delta and vega](05-simm-qualifying-credit.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-qualifying-credit-delta-and-vega) distinguish exact factor netting from concentration grouping and preserve the linear residual charge.
- [Nonqualifying-credit delta and vega](06-simm-nonqualifying-credit.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-nonqualifying-credit-delta-and-vega) require dated classification and distinguish tranche concentration from classified correlation groups.
- [Equity delta and vega](07-simm-equity.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-equity-delta-and-vega) require dated factor classification, a bucket-bound volatility proxy and explicit residual addition.
- [FX delta and pair vega](08-simm-fx.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-fx-delta-and-pair-vega) use directed USD shocks, both-leg proxy weights, separate concentration categories and checked signed dependence.
- [Commodity delta and vega](09-simm-commodity.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-commodity-delta-and-vega) preserve exact within-bucket factors and place bucket 16 inside the 17-bucket quadratic.
- [Expiry scaling and curvature](10-simm-curvature.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-simm-expiry-scaling-and-curvature-aggregation) bind the signed side and qualified expiries, use squared base dependence and retain complete charge evidence.
- SIMM assembly preserves product scope, complete applicability and approximation status at the supported historical USD horizon. [SIMM assembly](11-simm-total-and-mpor.md#accepted-decision-for-product-class-simm-assembly-and-supported-horizons).
- Gross schedule IM applies an exact profile rate to qualified effective notional in its currency at the reference horizon. [Gross schedule calculation](12-schedule-notional.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-gross-schedule-initial-margin).
- Schedule netting applies one reduction to the eligible set and preserves full gross IM if a nonempty set has no positive replacement marks. [Schedule netting](13-schedule-net-gross.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-schedule-netting-and-reduction).
- Cleared IM uses a qualified reported requirement or an explicitly elected indicative profile at its own horizon. [Cleared IM routes](14-ccp-external-and-proxy.md#accepted-decision-for-elected-cleared-initial-margin-routes).
- Collateral valuation uses one strict eligibility lookup and credits the checked value after the governed haircut. [Collateral eligibility and credited value](15-collateral-eligibility-and-haircuts.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-collateral-eligibility-and-credited-value).
- Collateral concentration applies every elected cap to the full group on the same basis as the eligible pool. [Collateral concentration](16-collateral-concentration.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-collateral-pool-concentration).
- IM account terms use the elected threshold and MTA once per exact one-way account. [IM account terms](17-csa-selection-and-im-terms.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-csa-elections-and-one-way-im-account-terms).
- VM applies signed exposure and credited collateral with elected side thresholds, MTA and transfer rounding. [Signed VM](18-variation-margin.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-signed-variation-margin-and-transfers).
- VM replay uses elected effective call slots and credits actual and agreed pending collateral under the elected policy. [VM call and settlement replay](19-margin-dates-and-replay.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-vm-schedules-and-settlement-replay).
- Repo cash margin uses the elected collateral target, settled refund capacity and signed interest on settled balances. [Repo cash margin](20-repo-margin-cashflows.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-repo-cash-margin-and-interest).
- Collateral metrics use exact adequacy, report ratios only for positive requirements and preserve the static funding and haircut bases. [Collateral metrics](21-collateral-metrics.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-collateral-metrics).
- Margin tables and wire state retain the typed calculation basis, checked source identity and explicit result qualification. [Margin wire and provenance](22-wire-tables-and-provenance.md#accepted-decision-for-checked-margin-tables-wire-state-and-provenance).
- Confirmed static concerns include row-level concentration checking, incomplete override-domain guards, ordinary row-import validation gaps and calendar-day settlement diagnostics labelled as business days. These static observations do not establish numerical consequences or authorize contract changes.
